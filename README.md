# CardEdge

**Wall Street tools for the sports card market.**

The sports card market has matured into a multi-billion dollar asset class. The tooling hasn't. Collectors and dealers are making six and seven-figure decisions using spreadsheets, gut feel, and Facebook groups — while institutional investors use quantitative models, real-time signals, and disciplined portfolio management.

CardEdge closes that gap. It is not a dashboard. It is not a price tracker. It is an active investment platform built around a single question: **what should you do right now, and why?**

---

## The Problem

Every major card app today is descriptive. They show you what a card sold for. They show you what your collection is worth. They stop there.

That's not investing. That's record-keeping.

Real investment decisions require understanding fair value, population dynamics, seasonal timing, player trajectory, grade arbitrage, and exit strategy — all at once, in real time. No platform synthesizes these signals into actionable guidance. CardEdge is built to do exactly that.

---

## Core Capabilities

### Deal Discovery
Live scanning across marketplaces for cards priced below fair value, with ROI thresholds you define. Cross-platform arbitrage detection. Grade arbitrage identification — raw cards where the grading math is clearly profitable. Alerts that fire in real time, not digests.

### Acquisition Intelligence
Fair value modeling that accounts for recent sales volume, trend direction, population size, and seasonal patterns — not just the last three comps. Pop-adjusted valuations that reflect what a population change actually means for price. Buy now vs. wait signals grounded in historical pattern data.

### Portfolio Intelligence
True cost basis tracking inclusive of all fees, grading costs, and shipping. Population monitoring with sell-trigger alerts when your cards' grades are at risk of dilution. Player momentum tracking correlated to price movement. Concentration risk scoring to manage exposure across players, sports, and grade tiers.

### Exit Optimization
Sell signal generation that synthesizes cost basis, pop trajectory, player news, and seasonal timing into a clear recommendation with full reasoning. Platform recommendations by card type and target price. Tax lot optimization for collectors managing multiple acquisitions of the same card.

### Performance Accounting
Real ROI — realized and unrealized — with IRR calculation. Capital velocity tracking to surface how efficiently money is cycling. Win/loss breakdowns by player, sport, set, and grade tier to identify where your actual edge is. Benchmark comparison against overall market performance.

---

## Design Principle

Every feature in CardEdge exists to answer one of three questions:

- Should I buy this?
- Should I hold this?
- Should I sell this now?

Features that don't move one of those needles don't ship.

---

## The Moat

CardEdge gets sharper the more it's used. Buy and sell decisions tracked through the platform — with actual outcomes recorded — feed models that improve signal quality over time. Cost basis data creates high switching costs. The investor-first framing attracts a user who is underserved by every existing product in the market: the person treating cards as a real asset class, not a hobby.

---

## Status

Early development. Features being built and validated individually. Architecture prioritizes depth over breadth — each capability is designed to be genuinely better than the current alternative, not a feature checkbox.

---

## Vision

The sports card market deserves the same analytical rigor applied to equities, real estate, and alternative assets. CardEdge is building the infrastructure to make that possible — starting with the tools that generate real returns for the people using them today.
